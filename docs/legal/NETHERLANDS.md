# Netherlands — Jurisdiction Legal Research

Scope: digital content subscriptions, social profiles/messaging, general paid/premium features, platform operator licensing. **Out of scope by project decision:** independent sex-worker advertising, service booking, sexual-service payment brokering (module D). Note for clarity only: the Netherlands has a long-standing legalized, municipally-licensed prostitution/brothel regime (since the 2000 brothel-ban repeal); this project does not build features for that activity and this fact was not researched further.

No item below is a permission to operate. `APPROVED` is never self-assigned by research — it requires actual legal counsel review, applicable permits, and a recorded operator decision.

### 1. Legal status of adult digital content platforms (OnlyFans-style)
**Finding:** No Netherlands-specific statute targeting creator-subscription platforms as a category was found; general Dutch e-commerce/consumer law plus the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) apply. The EU Commission's March 2026 preliminary DSA findings against Pornhub/XNXX for inadequate (self-declared) age verification are EU-wide and bind any Netherlands-accessible adult-content surface equally.

### 6. Foreign company ownership
**Finding:** Commercial-formation-provider consensus: no nationality restriction on BV ownership or directorship; the standard route for a non-resident founder is a notarial deed executed via a legalized/apostilled Power of Attorney, letting the founder stay abroad while a Dutch civil-law notary executes incorporation.
**Source:** [Intercompany Solutions — Dutch BV formation remote setup 2026](https://intercompanysolutions.com/professional-guide-to-dutch-bv-formation-remote-setup-in-2026/); [Foreign Director of a Dutch BV 2026](https://intercompanysolutions.com/blog/foreign-director-of-a-dutch-bv-legal-requirements-and-remote-setup-2026/) · Accessed 2026-10-27. **All sources found are the same commercial provider — no independent or official (KVK/government) confirmation obtained.**
**Status:** REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW (single-vendor-source risk). **Review needed:** Yes.

### 10. Taxes relevant to a digital platform business
**Finding:** Standard Dutch VAT (BTW) rate is 21%, applying to B2C digital services, with the same EU-wide €10,000 cross-border threshold and One-Stop-Shop (OSS) reporting mechanism as other EU states. Vendor-quoted corporate income tax: 19% up to €200,000 profit, 25.8% above that (2026 figures per vendor, not independently verified against the Belastingdienst). KVK registration fee quoted at €85.15; minimum share capital €0.01 (both vendor-sourced).
**Source:** [Intercompany Solutions — 2026 Non-Resident FAQ](https://intercompanysolutions.com/blog/netherlands-company-registration-2026-non-resident-faq/) · Accessed 2026-10-27. Not cross-checked against belastingdienst.nl or kvk.nl.
**Status:** REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW (single-vendor-source figures). **Review needed:** Yes.
