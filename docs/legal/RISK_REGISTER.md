# Risk Register — Phase 1 Research

Prioritized by actual urgency/severity, not alphabetically. Covers legal, payment-infrastructure,
and competitive-landscape risk surfaced across `docs/legal/*.md`, `docs/payments/PROVIDER_COMPARISON.md`,
and `docs/research/COMPETITORS.md`. Nothing here is a legal opinion; "status" reflects research
state, not a cleared/approved state. Accessed date: 2026-10-27.
